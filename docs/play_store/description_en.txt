Full description:

Not sure how much that jacket really costs in Berlin? Or that bottle of wine in Barcelona?

PRZevolut is your smart AR currency converter. Just point your phone's camera at any price tag – the app instantly reads the amount and overlays the conversion to Polish Złoty right on your screen.

✨ KEY FEATURES:

📷 AR Price Scanner
Point your camera at any price label. Our OCR technology reads the amount and you see an instant PLN conversion overlaid on screen. Supported currencies: EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, CZK.

💱 Live NBP Exchange Rates
Rates pulled directly from the National Bank of Poland (NBP), updated automatically throughout the day. Always accurate, always reliable.

✈️ Works Offline
Traveling abroad without data? No problem – the app stores the latest rates locally and works without an internet connection.

🔔 Currency Rate Alerts
Set a target rate for EUR or USD and get notified the moment it hits your level. Perfect for planning currency exchanges.

🔐 Secure Account
Log in with your password or fingerprint. Your alerts sync across devices.

📊 Conversion History
Revisit amounts you scanned earlier – your last 20 conversions are always available.

---

Why does PRZevolut require a smartphone?
• Camera – for real-time AR price scanning (impossible on a desktop)
• Offline mode – abroad, internet can be expensive or unavailable; local rate storage ensures the app works without roaming
• Push notifications – currency alerts need to reach a mobile device the user carries everywhere

---

Privacy:
• Camera images are NEVER sent to a server – OCR runs fully on-device (Google ML Kit)
• Passwords are hashed – never stored in plain text
• No location data collected
